              How to read this map

              Every score runs from 1 to 5 and belongs to one of two lenses.

              Implementation

              How much AI governance is in force and operating: the implementation index, regulation status, policy lever and enforcement level.

              1 means no observable activity. 5 means the rules are in force, the bodies are staffed and acting, and enforcement is routine and published.

              Governance style

              How a country governs AI, not how well. Governance type runs from centralised (1) to distributed (5); actor involvement from narrow (1) to broad (5). Neither end is better.

              Frontier risk governance

              How close a country’s governance of catastrophic risk from frontier AI comes to a standard stated in the methodology: whether the state can see, test and stop a dangerous frontier model. It is not part of the implementation index.

              Each country is scored on a track. A frontier host, where a developer of a frontier-scale model is based, is scored on four sub-indicators; a compute or chokepoint country, with frontier-scale compute or a node of the advanced-chip supply chain, on three; every other country, on the global track, on two. A sub-indicator outside the track reads “Not applicable”, never a 1.

              The score is the mean of the sub-indicators that apply, capped at one point above the weakest of them, so a high mark on one element cannot cover for a missing one.

              It is an audit against a stated standard, not a measure of how safe a country is. How the frontier lens is scored

              A higher score is not a better score. The map shows how much is in force, not whether the rules are good, fair or effective.

              The implementation index is the mean of the three implementation dimensions. Each dimension is the mean of four sub-indicators: open one in a country’s entry to see each score and what its level means.

              Every entry has a confidence rating for how well its sources support it. Countries rated low confidence are hatched on the map (switch this off under Filter). Grey countries have no data.
